Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

Enlevel Dungeon does not collect any personal information.

1. Information we collect

The game is played without sign-up or login, and collects no personal information of any kind, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, location or device identifiers. It does not include any third-party SDKs that collect personal information, such as advertising, analytics or payment processing SDKs.

2. Information stored on your device

Game progress (levels, upgrades, relics and so on) and settings (such as volume) are stored only on your device and are never sent to any external server. Deleting the app also deletes this information.

3. Sharing with third parties

Because we collect no personal information, we do not share it with or entrust its processing to any third party. Purchases are made through the store you install the game from (Google Play or the App Store), and any information handled in that process is governed by the store's own policy: the Google Privacy Policy or the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Children's privacy

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a feature that collects personal information is added, we will announce it on this page in advance along with the new effective date.
